GTBank Fashion Weekend

Client

GTBank

Year

2026

Services

Content Direction, Videography, Social Media

Rationale

GTBank Fashion Weekend brings together some of the most exciting names in African and international fashion for a live runway show, but a show only lasts a night — the goal of this work was to make it last far longer than that online. The brief was to turn raw runway footage into short, high-energy films that could carry the show's momentum across social and digital before, during, and after the event.

Working from footage captured on the runway, including standout walks from designers like Xu Zhi and Tolu C, the focus was on pace and rhythm rather than a straight document of the show. Each cut was edited to hit hard in the first few seconds, built around the beat drops, silhouette reveals, and crowd energy that make fashion film worth stopping to watch on a feed rather than scrolling past.

Multiple versions were produced from the same footage — different cuts and an alternate soundtrack edit — so the content could flex across placements: a moodier version for the brand's owned channels, a punchier version for paid promotion, and designer-specific runway cuts that could be shared directly with Xu Zhi, Tolu C, and other participating labels to extend reach through their own audiences.

The result is a set of promo films designed less as a recap and more as an invitation — content built to make people who weren't in the room feel the pull of the runway, and to keep GTBank Fashion Weekend visible and talked about well beyond the night of the show.
