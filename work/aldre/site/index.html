    02 — The ritual

    We blend in small harvests, pack by hand, and print every tin in a run of three hundred. Then we wait for the next season.

    MEASUREOne heaped spoon, 3g, for every cup.
    STEEP80–95°C water, two to four minutes. Then taste.
    REPEATGood leaves open three times. Keep pouring.
