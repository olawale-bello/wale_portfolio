OSSO is a Copenhagen-based specialty roastery
with craft and storytelling at our core.

Read more

We trace your coffee to its very origin, find its story,
and let the cup translate it to your guests.

Whether you are a neighbourhood café or a hotel group, we source, roast and translate what makes your cup unique.

01

Source

Direct relationships with growers in Ethiopia, Colombia and Kenya, visited every harvest.

02

Roast

Small batches on a restored 1960s drum roaster, profiled to bring out each origin.

03

Serve

Training, equipment and recipes so every cup tastes the way it should.
